GTA Codes

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

GTA Codes ("the app", "we", "us") is an unofficial, fan-made reference guide for video-game cheat codes. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. By using the app you agree to this policy.

In short: GTA Codes does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information. It shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. Your saved settings stay on your device.

Information we collect

We do not ourselves collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The app has no sign-in and no user profiles. However, the app shows ads using Google AdMob, and AdMob collects certain data to serve and measure those ads (see "Advertising" below).

Data stored on your device

The app saves a few preferences locally on your device only (using Android's on-device storage):

  • Your favourited cheats
  • Your preferred controller (PlayStation or Xbox)
  • Your chosen theme (System, Light, or Dark)

This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us or anyone else, and is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Network requests

To keep its cheat list and images up to date, the app downloads public content files (cheat data and images) from third-party hosting. These requests are standard web requests and do not include any personal identifiers from you. As with any internet request, the hosting providers may automatically log technical information such as your IP address and device/browser type in their own server logs, under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub (content hosting)
  • Pexels (royalty-free images)

Advertising

GTA Codes displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general app-usage/interaction data. This is handled by Google under its own policies; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before serving personalised ads. You can reset or limit ad personalisation at any time in your device settings under Google › Ads (reset advertising ID / opt out of ads personalisation). The app uses no other analytics or usage-tracking tools.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. No personal data is gathered from users of any age.

Changes to this policy
